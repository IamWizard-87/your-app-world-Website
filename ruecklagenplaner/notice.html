Rücklagenplaner is a planning tool

The app works only with the figures you enter yourself. It retrieves no account data, verifies nothing and knows neither your actual transactions nor any payments due.

No advice

What the app shows is not investment, tax or legal advice, and not a recommendation to buy or sell financial products. For decisions with financial consequences please seek expert advice.

Care with your entries

The figure shown as “Available” is only as reliable as the balance you last entered. Update it regularly, and bear in mind that payments already instructed but not yet booked may not be included in the balance your bank shows.
