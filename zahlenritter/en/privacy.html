The short version

Zahlenritter is a maths game for children that runs entirely on the device. There are no user accounts, no registration, no server operated by us, no advertising, no in-app purchases, no tracking and no third-party analytics. The app makes no network connections.

The app collects no personal data about your child or about you. We never have access to any data from the app.

Processing on the device

Only the saved game is stored, and only locally on the device:

  • Which levels are unlocked and how many coins were earned per level
  • The number of numbers recovered and the stickers collected
  • Whether the opening story has already been shown
  • The chosen language and whether sound and read-aloud are switched on

The saved game contains no names, no photos, no location data and no identifiers. It does not leave the device; there is no iCloud synchronisation. A backup of the device may include it — that is up to your iOS settings.

Read-aloud and sounds

The app reads all texts aloud using the iOS speech output. Speech output runs on the device; the sentences read aloud are not transmitted. The app's sounds are also generated on the device.

The app uses neither the microphone nor the camera, and neither photos, location nor contacts.

No identifiers, no advertising

The app uses no advertising identifier (IDFA), does not ask for App Tracking Transparency and embeds no ad networks. There is no profile, no cross-app recognition and no disclosure to third parties.

Children

Zahlenritter is made for children aged about five to seven. The app contains no advertising, no purchase offers and no links to the internet. The "Parents" area containing these texts is protected by a maths question for grown-ups.

App Store

Downloading and, where applicable, purchasing the app are handled exclusively by Apple. We receive only aggregated, non-personal sales and payout reports from Apple. We neither collect nor store payment data.

The service is provided by Apple Inc., One Apple Park Way, Cupertino, CA 95014, USA, and Apple Distribution International Ltd., Cork, Ireland. Apple's privacy policy applies: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR.

Diagnostic data

The app embeds no crash or analytics services of its own. If your device sends diagnostic data to Apple, this is based on your consent in the iOS system settings ("Analytics & Improvements"). At most we receive anonymised crash reports through App Store Connect.

Contacting support

If you write to our support address we process your details — sender address, subject, message text and any attachments — solely to handle your enquiry and any follow-up questions. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR; our legitimate interest lies in answering you. Where the enquiry concerns a purchase or its initiation, Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR applies in addition.

We do not run the support mailbox ourselves: it is hosted by Google (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). Your message sits in our mailbox there and is read and answered by us personally.

No AI service is involved. The contents of your message are neither evaluated automatically nor used to train AI models; there is no automated decision-making within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR.

Google acts as our processor under Art. 28 GDPR; a data processing agreement is in place.

What you write to us is up to you. A support enquiry needs neither details about your child nor your real name; you are welcome to write from a neutral address. Please do not send photos or screenshots in which your child can be recognised.

Messages are deleted as soon as they are no longer needed, at the latest once statutory retention periods have expired.

Recipients and third countries

We do not pass on any data from the game — we have no access to it. Personal data reaches recipients only in the cases described above:

  • Apple — when the app is downloaded or purchased
  • Google — operation of the support mailbox

With both, processing may also take place in the USA. Apple and Google are certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; the European Commission's standard contractual clauses apply in addition.

Retention and deletion

The saved game remains stored until the app is deleted from the device, which removes it completely. Note that this also removes the unlocked levels and the collected stickers.

Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21 GDPR). We hold no data about you or your child from the app itself; any access request can therefore only be answered in the negative. If you have written to us, these rights concern that correspondence in the support mailbox.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), in particular the authority of your habitual residence, your place of work or the place of the alleged infringement.

Changes

We adapt this policy when changes to the app make that necessary. The current version is available in the app in the "Parents" area and at https://your-app-world.de/zahlenritter/en/privacy.html
