Frequently asked questions

How do I get to the "Parents" area?

On the start screen, tap "Parents" at the top left and answer the multiplication question. There you will find the legal notice, privacy policy, terms of use, the support address and the version number.

Nothing is read aloud.

Check that sound is switched on on the start screen (a music note rather than a crossed-out speaker) and that the device is not muted or turned right down. The voice comes from the iOS speech output; better voices for German and English can be downloaded in the iOS settings under "Accessibility".

How do I change the language?

Choose "DE" or "EN" at the top right of the start screen. Texts are then read aloud in that language; the saved game is kept.

My child is stuck on a sum.

After the second mistake the game shows a counting aid with dots. A sum solved with help comes back again at the end of the level. If all three hearts are gone, the level simply starts again – without any penalty.

What happens after the final battle?

After the rescue, "Play again" starts a new round: the map begins at the village again, and the stickers collected stay in the album.

Is the saved game backed up?

The saved game lives only on the device; there is no account and no cloud sync. It is backed up with the iOS backup. If the app is deleted, the progress is gone.

Are there purchases or ads in the app?

No. The app contains no advertising, no purchase offers and no subscription.

How do I get a refund?

App Store purchases are handled exclusively by Apple; we cannot refund them technically. Request a refund directly from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
