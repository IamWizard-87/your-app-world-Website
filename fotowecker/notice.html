Fotowecker is not a backup

When you add a photo to a reminder, Fotowecker stores a **copy** of it. The original stays where it was – in your photo library, in the message or in the file you shared it from.

Please do not delete your originals just because an image is in Fotowecker. The app is a reminder tool, not an archive and not a backup.

What that means in practice

  • If you delete the app, the copies inside it are gone.
  • If you delete a reminder, its photos go with it – on all your devices.
  • iCloud sync is not a backup: it mirrors every change. What you delete on one device disappears on the others too.
  • For a real backup use the iOS backup, iCloud Photos or a copy on your computer.

Notifications may not arrive

Whether a reminder appears is decided by iOS. A switched-off device, revoked permissions, a focus setting or full storage can mean a notification comes late or not at all. Reminders that are due are always listed in the app under "Due" – even when no notification appeared.

For anything with a deadline (warranty, cancellation, tax documents) do not rely on Fotowecker alone; add it to a calendar as well.
