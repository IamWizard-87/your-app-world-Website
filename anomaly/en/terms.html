Free play, full version and hint packs

The app can be played free of charge. Without any purchase you can play the difficulties "Introductory", "Easy" and "Medium" in free play — each once you have earned it — as well as the daily puzzle on every weekday, including the days it falls on "Hard" or "Extreme". Hints come from a daily allowance and from credit you earn by solving cases. Beyond that there are two kinds of in-app purchase:

  • The full version unlocks the difficulties "Hard" and "Extreme" in free play and lifts the limit on hints permanently. It is a one-time purchase and can be brought to another device on the same Apple ID at any time with "Restore purchases".
  • Hint packs credit a fixed number of hints. They are consumables.

Earned progression is unaffected: even with the full version, a difficulty only opens once the cases of the previous one are solved.

There is no subscription. Nothing renews automatically and nothing is charged recurrently.

Where purchased hints live

Purchased hints that you have not used yet are stored only on the device where they were bought. The game keeps no user account and no cloud storage. It follows that they do not travel to a second device, and that they are lost if you delete the app or change devices. Under App Store rules, consumables cannot be restored.

If you play on several devices, or would rather not have to think about this, the full version is the better fit — it belongs to your Apple ID and can always be brought back.
