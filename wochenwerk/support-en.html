Frequently asked questions

How do I share a calendar with my family?

Open the calendar, tap “Invite people” and send the link from the system share sheet. The invited person taps the link and the calendar appears for them under “Shared with me”. Each side needs their own Apple Account with iCloud turned on.

Does everyone in the family have to buy Wochenwerk Pro?

No. Accepting an invitation and working fully in a shared calendar is always free. Pro is only needed by the person who creates calendars and invites others.

What happens when my subscription expires?

Nothing disappears. All calendars, people, events and timetables stay readable, editable and exportable. You simply cannot create new calendars, people, events and school days beyond the free allowance any more.

Why don't lessons show during the holidays?

Because a holiday period has been entered – that is exactly what it is for. The other way round: the app does not know your school holidays by itself. Enter them under “Timetable → Holidays”, otherwise lessons will show during the first week off as well.

Maths is cancelled today – should I delete the lesson?

No. Tap the lesson in the calendar and choose “Just this day”. Deleting the lesson would remove it from the whole term, including the past.

How do A/B weeks work?

Each lesson applies either every week or only in A or B weeks. Which week is currently A is set by the anchor in the timetable – you set it once, on a week you are sure about.

Does the app sync between several devices?

Yes, as long as iCloud is turned on. The data lives in the private iCloud database of your Apple Account. Without iCloud the app keeps working purely locally – but then there are no shared calendars.

I bought Wochenwerk Pro but cannot see the features.

Tap “More → Restore purchases” and make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I get my events out again?

Every calendar can be exported as an .ics file under its name – in the format every calendar app reads. Export is never locked. The timetable is not included; .ics cannot carry it honestly.

How do I delete all data?

“More → Delete all data” removes every calendar you created yourself – on all your devices and for everyone you shared it with. Calendars other people shared with you are left individually, from the calendar's own screen.
