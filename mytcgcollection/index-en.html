Your card collection, sorted. Your decks, legal.

My TCG Collection keeps your physical collection in order and helps you build decks – with the official deck sizes, the copy limit and the current banlist. Your data stays on your device.

Why not just a spreadsheet?

Because a spreadsheet does not know that “Pot of Greed” is forbidden, that Synchro monsters belong in the Extra Deck, and that three prints of the same card are still only three copies of one card. The app does.

What the app does

Manage your collection

Add cards by their card number, with edition and a comment per copy. Filter by category, type, attribute, level, rarity, archetype and more. Free up to 50 cards, unlimited with Pro.

Deck building with rule checks (Pro)

Main Deck 40–60, Extra Deck and Side Deck 0–15 each. Cards sort themselves: Fusion, Synchro, Xyz and Link go to the Extra Deck automatically.

Copy limit and banlist

At most three copies of a card across all deck sections together – and the TCG banlist overrides that: forbidden, limited and semi-limited are taken into account.

What you own

Decks may contain cards you do not have yet. The app marks them as “Owned 2/3” instead of blocking you – planning before buying.

Statistics

Distribution by card type, type, attribute, level, rank, Link Rating and rarity. Plus the estimated collection value.

Import and export

Read existing lists in from an Excel file and export your collection as Excel at any time – both without a purchase. Creating the complete backup file is part of Pro, as is the automatic backup to your iCloud. Restoring one always works, with or without Pro.

Offline and without an account

There is no registration, no user account and no server operated by the provider. Your collection sits in a database on your device. The app already ships with the card database, so it works right away and without a connection.

It needs an internet connection only when you update the card database or look up an unknown card number. The details are in the privacy policy.

Pro and the tip jar

Pro

For free you keep up to 50 cards, see the full statistics, read Excel lists in and export your collection as Excel. Pro removes the card limit and unlocks the deck builder, the wishlist and creating complete backups – including an automatic backup to your own iCloud that even survives deleting the app. Restoring an existing backup works at any time, with or without Pro. No advertising, no tracking.

Whatever you have already recorded stays visible and exportable – even once a subscription ends. Only new cards stop being added.

Three ways, the same unlock:

  • Monthly subscription – cancellable every month
  • Yearly subscription – saves around half
  • One-time purchase – not a subscription, stays for good

The current prices are shown in the App Store and in the app. Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel. Billing goes through your Apple Account and is charged within 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can cancel at any time in your Apple Account settings, at the latest 24 hours before it renews. The one-time purchase is not a subscription and does not renew.

Tip jar

If you would like to support development, you can leave something voluntarily. That is a pure tip: it unlocks nothing and is never required to use the app.

System requirements

iPhone with iOS 17 or newer. On the iPad the app runs in iPhone mode.
