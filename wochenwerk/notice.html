Important notice

This is a translation. The binding version is the German one; in case of doubt, the German text prevails.

Wochenwerk is a planning tool

The app shows only what you, and the people you share with, enter yourselves. It verifies nothing, retrieves no data from schools or clubs, and does not know whether an appointment actually takes place.

It is no substitute for talking to each other

A change reaches the other family members only once their devices have synced it. That normally happens within seconds, but it depends on iCloud, on the network and on the settings of each device. For short-notice and important changes, do not rely on the calendar alone.

Public holidays and school holidays

The app calculates the statutory public holidays for each German state from fixed rules. Regional particularities – such as holidays that apply only in individual municipalities – are not included. **You enter school holidays and days off yourself**; the app does not know them and can only cancel lessons on those days if the period has been entered.

Timetable

The timetable shows the rule, not reality: substitutions, cancellations and room changes appear only once they are entered. At the start of each term, check that the period grid and the validity periods are still correct.
