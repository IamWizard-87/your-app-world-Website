The most important point first

Wochenwerk is an on-device app with iCloud syncing. There are no user accounts with the developer, no registration, no server run by the developer, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party analytics tools. Everything you enter – calendars, people, events, timetables, homework and notes – stays on your device and, if you use iCloud, in the iCloud of your Apple Account.

The developer has no access to this data at any time.

Processing on the device

The following data is stored on your device:

  • The names and colours of your calendars
  • The names, colours and symbols of the people you create
  • The titles, times, locations, notes, links, reminders and recurrence rules of your events
  • The period grid, subjects, teacher names, rooms, holiday periods and exceptions of the timetable
  • Homework and exams
  • App settings such as the first day of the week and the default calendar

This data leaves your device only if you use iCloud syncing, share a calendar, or start and pass on an export yourself.

iCloud syncing

If iCloud is turned on in the system settings, the app mirrors its data through the private iCloud database of your Apple Account (Apple CloudKit). The developer is technically unable to access this private database.

The provider of the service is Apple Inc., One Apple Park Way, Cupertino, CA 95014, USA, or Apple Distribution International Ltd., Cork, Ireland. Apple's privacy policy applies: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR – syncing is part of the function you requested. You can turn it off for this app at any time in the system settings under “Apple Account → iCloud”.

So that changes arrive promptly, Apple sends your devices silent hints about new data (push). They contain no content from your events and do not appear on screen.

Shared calendars

When you share a calendar, **you** invite other people through Apple's sharing. From the moment they accept, those people see the entire content of that calendar – events, people, timetable and notes – and, depending on the access you grant, can change it as well. The disclosure originates with you; you are responsible for choosing whom you invite and for what you put into a shared calendar.

Technically this runs through Apple's CloudKit sharing. About the invited people the app shows what Apple passes to it: name, role, access right and acceptance status, and, for invitations not yet accepted, the e-mail address or phone number you entered. The developer does not process these details; they are displayed on your device only.

You can end a share at any time, remove individual participants, or leave a calendar shared with you.

Data about children

A family calendar usually contains details about children: first names, school lessons, appointments. You enter these yourself; they stay on your devices, in your iCloud and – if you share a calendar – with the people you invited. The developer does not receive them and does not analyse them.

The app is aimed at adults who run their family's calendar. It requires no account and no sign-in, and collects no data for advertising purposes.

Calendar access (import from Apple Calendar)

If you import events from Apple Calendar, the app asks for the system permission required for that. The access serves solely to read the calendars you select, for the period you choose. The events are copied into your Wochenwerk calendar; the app writes nothing back to Apple Calendar. You can withdraw the permission in the system settings at any time; importing from an `.ics` file is unaffected by this.

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR.

Importing and exporting files

When importing an `.ics` file and when exporting a calendar, you choose the file yourself, or decide yourself where it is written. The processing takes place on your device; no transmission to the developer occurs.

App Store and in-app purchases

Purchase, payment and management of in-app purchases are handled exclusively by Apple. From Apple the developer receives only aggregated, non-personal sales and payout reports. Payment data is neither collected nor stored by the developer.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR.

Notifications

Reminders for events are delivered by your own device. The app works out the next reminder times from your events and hands them to your device's notification system; nothing leaves the device in the process, and the provider operates no push service. The title and start of the event are part of the notification and are therefore visible on the lock screen unless you change that in the system settings. You grant permission for notifications to the system and can withdraw it there at any time; without it nothing is scheduled. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR, as the reminder is a function you asked for.

This is separate from the silent notifications Apple uses to tell your devices about new iCloud data; those belong to syncing and display nothing.

Diagnostic data

The app includes no crash reporting or analytics services of its own. If your device sends diagnostic data to Apple, that is based on your consent in the system settings (“Analytics & Improvements”). At most the developer receives anonymised crash reports from it through App Store Connect.

Contact and support

If you write us a support e-mail, we process your details – sender address, subject, message text and any attachments – exclusively to deal with your enquiry and in case of follow-up questions. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR; our legitimate interest lies in answering your enquiry. If the enquiry concerns a purchase or the run-up to one, Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR applies in addition.

We do not run the support mailbox ourselves: it is hosted by Google (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). Your message sits in our mailbox there and is read and answered exclusively by us personally.

No AI service is involved. The contents of your message are neither analysed automatically nor used to train AI models; there is no automated decision-making in individual cases within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR.

Google acts as our processor under Art. 28 GDPR; a corresponding data processing agreement is in place.

What you write to us is up to you. To deal with a support enquiry we need no children's names, no schools, no addresses and no complete lists of appointments – please redact such details in screenshots. For sensitive matters you are welcome to write to us without your real name, from a neutral address.

Messages are deleted as soon as they are no longer needed, and at the latest once statutory retention periods have expired.

Recipients and third countries

We do not pass on the data you enter in the app – we have no access to it. Personal data has recipients only in the cases described above:

  • Apple – where iCloud syncing is turned on, for shared calendars, and for purchase and billing
  • The people you invite – for shared calendars
  • Google – operation of the support mailbox

With Apple and Google, processing may also take place in the USA. Both are certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; in addition the European Commission's standard contractual clauses apply.

Retention and deletion

Your data stays stored until you delete it. You delete individual entries directly in the app. All local data is removed when you delete the app from your device. Data synced through iCloud is additionally removed in the system settings under “Apple Account → iCloud → Manage Storage”. If you have shared a calendar, the data stays with the participants until you end the share or delete the calendar.

Your rights

You have the rights to information (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21 GDPR). We hold no data about you from the app itself; to that extent we can only answer a request in the negative. If you have written to us, these rights concern the correspondence in the support mailbox.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint about the processing with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). In particular you may contact the authority of your habitual residence, your place of work or the place of the alleged infringement.

Changes

We adapt this statement when changes to the app make that necessary. The current version is available in the app under “More → Privacy” and at https://your-app-world.de/wochenwerk/privacy.html
