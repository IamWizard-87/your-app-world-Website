Something aboard is off.

Anomaly is a deduction puzzle in the tradition of Einstein's riddle, dressed as a mystery on a research station in orbit. A handful of log entries, one grid, exactly one solution — and no luck involved.

How a log entry reads

Log order: lab → handheld scanner

Adjacent windows: Sørn ↔ inventory

The first sign says the direction is part of the statement: the lab comes before the handheld scanner. The second says the opposite — side by side, in either order. Telling those apart gets you half the station.

What the game is

Never a guess

Every case is built so that each step can be forced. There is exactly one solution, and a path to it that needs no trial and error.

Five difficulties

From introductory to extreme. The next one opens once you have solved three cases of the current level — at least one of them without a hint.

Daily puzzle

One case a day for everyone, with its own streak. The week is a ramp: easy on Monday, extreme on Sunday.

Hints that explain

Three stages: which log entry carries now, which cell follows from it, and finally filling it in. You pay for the look, not for the depth.

Consistency check

Is there a contradiction anywhere? The answer is exact — and limited for exactly that reason, with a cooldown instead of endless asking.

Bilingual and accessible

German and English, Dynamic Type up to the accessibility sizes, VoiceOver with spoken forms of every condensed value.

What the game is not

  • No advertising, no tracking, no analytics, no advertising identifier.
  • No accounts, no registration, no server — the game runs entirely offline.
  • No subscription. There is a one-time purchase and there are consumables, nothing else.
  • No energy meter that refills, and no waiting time you can buy away.

Hints and purchases

Hints come from three pools: a daily allowance, the credit that solved cases earn, and — if you like — purchased packs. They are spent top down: first what expires anyway, last what cost money.

The full version unlocks hints permanently and is a one-time purchase. Hint packs are consumables and stay on the device where they were bought — see the terms of use.
