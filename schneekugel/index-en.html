A snow globe with the faces that matter to you.

Choose up to four photos from your library – the app finds the faces itself, cuts them out and sets them on hand-drawn figures inside the globe. No cropping by hand.

What the app does

  • 18 scenes – nine German cities and nine landscapes, each one its own hand-drawn picture.
  • Four seasons – snow in winter, cherry blossom in spring, wheat fields in summer, golden leaves in autumn. Sky, ground and decoration change with them.
  • Share as a greeting – shake the globe, tap share, and the swirling snow becomes a short video with your own words.

Your photos stay with you

Portraits are processed entirely on your device – the cut-out runs through Apple's image recognition, with no detour via a server. There is no server operated by the provider, no user account, no advertising and no tracking.

Read the privacy policy

Free and premium

The app is free to use. A single one-off in-app purchase unlocks every scene and removes the watermark from shared greetings – there is no subscription. If you like, you can support the development with a voluntary contribution on top; it unlocks nothing and is never necessary.

Requirements

iPhone with iOS 18 or newer.
