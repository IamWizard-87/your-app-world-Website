“Dentist” is in the calendar. But whose?

Plenty of apps can share a calendar. None of them reliably says who an entry is for. Wochenwerk gives everyone in the family a profile with their own colour – and puts the name next to the event in plain text.

An event can belong to several people

The parents' evening belongs to Mum and Lina. In the grid it then carries both colours, in the list both names. An event without a person stays neutral in the calendar's colour – nobody is quietly assigned to it.

The colour is the fast information, the name the unambiguous one. That is why both are shown.

What the app does

People instead of guesswork

A profile with colour and symbol for each person – inside the shared calendar, not beside it. One tap filters the view down to a single child.

The timetable in the same calendar

Period grid, subjects, rooms, A/B weeks and validity periods. Lessons appear in the calendar in the child's colour – without creating hundreds of single events.

Holidays

The app calculates the statutory public holidays for all sixteen German states. School holidays are entered as a period; lessons are then cancelled automatically.

Homework and exams

On the due date, in the child's colour, tickable. Finished items leave the calendar and stay in the list.

Sharing through iCloud

Invite from the system share sheet. Changes appear on the other devices within seconds. Read-only access is possible.

Moving in without typing

Take over events straight from Apple Calendar or read an .ics file – with a preview and duplicate checking. Importing the same file twice creates nothing new.

Also

  • Five views – day, week, month, list, year – always showing the same set of events.
  • Editing a series asks whether you mean this event, all future ones, or all of them.
  • Export of every calendar as .ics – always free, with or without Pro.
  • iPhone, iPad and Mac on the same data; on the Mac with menu commands and keyboard shortcuts.
  • Dark mode, Dynamic Type and VoiceOver considered from the start.
  • No account, no sign-in, ready to use straight away.

What the app does not do

  • No user accounts, no registration, no advertising, no tracking.
  • No server of its own: your data lives on your devices and in your iCloud.
  • No school-holiday tables pulled from the internet – wrong holidays would make lessons vanish on school days.

Your data stays with you

Everything is stored on the device and synced through the private iCloud of your Apple Account. If you share a calendar, the sharing originates with you and runs through Apple's iCloud sharing. The developer has no access to it and runs no server.

That is why the App Store lists the app under “App Privacy” as “Data Not Collected”.
