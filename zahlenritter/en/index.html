# Zahlenritter

Sir Digit needs help with his sums.

The Count has stolen the Queen of Numbers – and all the numbers with her. Zahlenritter is a maths game for children aged about five to seven: every solved sum brings one number back.

3 + 4 = 7

Adding and subtracting with numbers up to nine. Every sentence in the game is read aloud, so children who cannot read confidently yet can still play along.

What the game offers

Five levels and a final battle

From the village across the bridge, the swamp and the tower stairs up to the throne room. At the end, five right answers in a row set the Queen free.

Mistakes are allowed

No red crosses, no time limit. After two wrong answers a counting aid with dots appears – and the sum comes back again later.

Tap, drag, count

Sometimes the answer is typed on a number pad, sometimes a number tile is dragged into place, sometimes barrels are counted.

Collect stickers

Every level completed earns a sticker for the album, plus coins depending on the number of mistakes.

German and English

Switch the language right on the start screen; texts are read aloud in the chosen language.

iPhone and iPad

Portrait only, iOS 17 or later. On iPad the game is shown centred at full screen height.

What the game does not do

  • No advertising, no purchase offers, no in-app purchases, no subscription.
  • No account, no registration, no server – the game runs entirely offline.
  • No tracking, no analytics, no links to the internet.
  • No microphone, no camera, no photos, no location.

For parents

The legal notice, privacy policy and contact details are also available in the app, in the "Parents" area on the start screen. It is protected by a multiplication question so that children do not end up there by accident.

The saved game stays on the device. See the privacy policy for details.

Questions?

Support goes to a person, not a ticket system.
